Driver's nights

Sleeping in the SUV is fine in Jammu, Srinagar and Manali. It is not fine at Kargil, Leh, Nubra, Pangong or Jispa in mid-October — the cabin will not hold heat overnight, and a cold, unslept driver on the 470 km day home is a risk to everyone. A bed at those nine nights is about ₹6,500 total. Budget it, and let him decide night by night.

This is the route for another year, not this one. Best window: depart between 10 and 20 September, after the monsoon has left Kinnaur and before Kunzum La sees its first snow. The hard truth about this route in October: Kunzum La and the Kaza–Gramphu road close on the first real snow, and that can be mid-month. Leave 10 October and you are at Kunzum on the 14th with no way out except back through Kinnaur. That is why it is the September route, and why Srinagar remains the October one.

Where the economy is

  1. One triple room where the property has a good one — Kargil, Nubra, Pangong. About ₹10,000 saved without forcing it on the nights that matter.
  2. Fill everything in Haryana before Jammu, and again in Kargil. Petrol is ₹4–6 cheaper on the plains; across 470 litres that is ₹2,000–2,500.
  3. The SUV is the kitchen. Kettle, stove, oats, eggs, tea, dry fruit. Breakfast and evening tea for four cuts the food line by a third, and at Pangong there is nothing to buy anyway.
  4. Driver on a flat trip rate, food included. ₹18,000–20,000 for fourteen days is a normal ask.
  5. Book direct by phone. Ladakh homestays run 10–15% cheaper than the apps in October and would rather have a confirmed guest than pay a commission.
  6. Jammu at the gurudwara sarai. Secure parking, langar, nominal donation.
  7. Do not touch the buffer day. Every unplanned day costs the group about ₹10,000. That is the biggest single variable, and it is why Srinagar in is cheaper than Manali in even though the kilometres are identical.

Before you ring anyone

  • Postpaid SIMs for all four people — prepaid will not work in J&K or Ladakh
  • Nubra, Pangong and Jispa confirmed in writing: open, heated, breakfast by 06:30
  • Leh guesthouse booked for four nights across two stays, bag-drop agreed
  • Driver's rate agreed flat, food included, cold-halt beds explained
  • Cash for everything past Kargil
  • Two jerry cans, oxygen cylinder rental arranged in Leh, oximeter bought
  • Passenger asked, not told, about the triple-room nights
